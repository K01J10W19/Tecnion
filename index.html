About the company

Technion LLC is a high-precision manufacturer specializing in turnkey surface-mount (SMD) PCB assembly. We deliver projects from our customers' drawings and technical specifications with maximum precision, consistent quality and on schedule.

We place 0402 components and larger with ±30–40 µm positioning control. A full quality-control cycle covers every assembly stage.

  • Made in Russia

    All our boards are assembled at our own production facility in St. Petersburg.

  • Quality standards

    We guarantee that every board meets all of its technical requirements.

Automated optical inspection

An inline AOI system checks every assembled board for missing, shifted or misoriented components and solder defects.

  • Inspection after reflow soldering
  • Detects bridges, tombstoning and insufficient solder
  • Defect reports tied to each board

Convection reflow oven

Multi-zone reflow soldering with precisely controlled thermal profiles.

  • Lead-free and tin-lead processes
  • Profiles tuned to each board and component set
  • Profile verification with thermocouple measurements
