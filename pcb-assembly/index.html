PCB assembly

We assemble printed circuit boards of any complexity on modern automated equipment, from incoming inspection of the bare board to functional testing of the finished product.

Assembly stages

  1. 01

    PCB preparation

    Incoming inspection of the board and its zones. Preparation for assembly according to the documentation.

  2. 02

    Solder paste application

    Stencil printing for fine pitch and dense assembly. Inspection of the printed paste.

  3. 03

    Component placement (SMT/THT)

    0201 / 0402 and dense layouts. BGA and complex packages to your specification.

  4. 04

    Soldering

    Reflow soldering for SMD. Wave soldering for THT when required.

  5. 05

    Functional testing

    Operability check and first power-up. Inspection against the customer's requirements.

Technologies:

  • Surface-mount technology (SMT)
  • Through-hole technology (THT)
  • Mixed assembly
  • Multilayer boards, including complex designs with blind and buried vias

Applied standards:

  • IPC-A-610
  • J-STD-001

The process delivers high reliability and compliance with industrial requirements for product quality and durability.
