About the company

Our goal is reliable, precise assembly of printed circuit boards of any complexity. We work with SMT, THT and mixed assembly, from first prototypes to small-batch production, and follow IPC-A-610 and J-STD-001 in every project.

We know that quality starts with a manufacturable design and ends with strict control at every stage of assembly and testing of the finished product. Our processes are built to minimize the risk of errors and keep quality consistent, even when working with high assembly density, BGA components and precision footprints.

Our key values:

  • Technical expertise

    We handle every type of assembly: surface-mount (SMT), through-hole (THT) and mixed. Our experience covers complex components where placement accuracy, soldering temperature control and the electrical properties of each joint are critical.

  • Responsibility for results

    Every order passes multi-stage inspection before it ships. We own the result and stay in touch until the device is running in your system.

  • An individual approach to complexity

    We take on projects with individual requirements: non-standard components, tight tolerances and special operating conditions. Each assembly is tailored to its task.

  • Transparency and openness

    We agree every stage, parameter and specification with you in advance, with no hidden costs and no surprises in the schedule.
